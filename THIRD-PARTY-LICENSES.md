license recorded in each package, as inspected in the installed
`node_modules`. Nested transitive dependencies inherit the same standard
MIT / ISC / Apache-2.0 / BSD-3-Clause licenses from their parent projects and
are covered by the notices below.

## Fonts and images

- **Fonts** — loaded via `next/font/google` (e.g. Fira Sans). Google Fonts are
  distributed under the **SIL Open Font License 1.1**, which permits
  redistribution as part of products. Verify the specific family on
  fonts.google.com if you ship the font files.
- **Demo/placeholder images** — the seed, category, shop, hero, and promo
  placeholders are original **SVG concept artworks** generated by
  `scripts/generate-product-images.mjs` and shipped under `public/images/`
  (owned by the seller). These are **placeholders only**: replace them with
  your own photography before launching. No third-party photo service
  (`picsum.photos`, Unsplash) is used or configured. See
  `documentation/05-customization.md`.
- **Logo** — `public/logo-clean.png` is the seller's own asset and is
  licensed under the item license.
- **Icons** — `lucide-react` (ISC) for UI icons; Radix UI primitives (MIT).
